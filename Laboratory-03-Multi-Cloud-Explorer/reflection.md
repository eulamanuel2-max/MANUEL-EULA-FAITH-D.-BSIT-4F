# Mission Reflection

Completing this multi-cloud exploration mission gave me a much clearer picture of how AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform actually differ in practice, not just in theory.

**Which cloud platform impressed me the most?**
AWS stood out the most because of the sheer breadth and maturity of its service catalog. Having been first to market, it offers a level of depth in areas like compute, storage, and AI/ML tooling that felt noticeably more developed than its competitors, along with extensive documentation and community support that made research easier.

**Similarities among AWS, Azure, and GCP**
Despite their differences, all three platforms share a common foundation: virtual machines, object storage, managed databases, identity and access management, and Kubernetes-based container orchestration. Each also follows a pay-as-you-go pricing model, offers a global network of data center regions, and provides a web-based management console alongside CLI and API access. This shared core makes the underlying skills transferable between providers.

**How business requirements influence platform selection**
No single platform is universally "best" — the right choice depends entirely on the client's context. A startup prioritizing low upfront cost and scalability needs different services than a university with an existing Microsoft ecosystem, or an AI research firm needing high-performance GPU/TPU compute. This mission reinforced that cloud selection is a business decision as much as a technical one.

**What I learned acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant**
Stepping into that role taught me to evaluate technology through the lens of client needs rather than personal preference, and to justify recommendations with concrete services and trade-offs rather than assumptions.

**Portfolio improvement**
My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio is now more organized and professional, with structured comparison tables, research documents, and clear commit history that better demonstrate my growing cloud competency.
